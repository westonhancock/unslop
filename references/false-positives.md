# False positives and human signals

Read this file before each detect task and each large edit. The most frequent error of an AI-cleanup skill is to remove good prose because the prose contains a word from a list.

## Not evidence of AI

A good human writer can use many patterns from the skill without AI. Each item below is not reliable evidence when it occurs alone.

- **Perfect grammar and a consistent style.** Many writers are professionals, or an editor examined their text. Polish is not evidence of AI.
- **A mix of informal and formal registers.** This mix usually comes from a person in a technical field, a young writer, or a person with neurodivergent writing habits. It does not usually come from a chatbot.
- **Dull or mechanical prose.** AI prose has *specific* patterns. If dull text does not have these patterns, it is only dull text.
- **Formal or academic vocabulary.** AI uses a specific set of formal words too much. It does not use all formal words too much. Do not change words such as "ostensibly" or "constituent" only because they sound academic.
- **One transition word alone.** *Additionally*, *moreover*, and *consequently* are AI patterns only when many occur together. One *however* is not an AI pattern.
- **Curly quotes alone.** macOS, Word, Google Docs, and most CMSes change straight quotes to curly quotes by default.
- **Em dashes alone.** Many editors and journalists use em dashes frequently. Em dashes are evidence only together with a formula rhythm that sounds like a sales text.
- **One short sentence for emphasis.** Human writers use short sentences to make a point strong. Identify a series of short sentences only when they occur together and make the tone too dramatic. A fragment is different: the sentence rules in `SKILL.md` always correct fragments outside quotes.
- **"Honestly" or "look" in the middle of a sentence.** These words are usual in informal writing. The AI pattern is the dramatic opener that stands alone.
- **A greeting or a signature in the style of a letter.** Writers used greetings and signatures for centuries before ChatGPT.
- **Claims without a source.** Most writing has no sources. A claim without a citation is not evidence.
- **A correct, complex format.** Visual editors and templates make a clean format without AI.
- **Text from another source.** Never rewrite a word from the lists in a quotation, a title, a proper name, a code block, or an example that *discusses* the word.

**Rule: find groups, not single patterns.** One em dash is not evidence. Em dashes, a rule-of-three triad, "vibrant tapestry," and a Conclusion section together are strong evidence.

## Signs of human writing to keep

When you see these signs, do not change the prose if possible. They are evidence of a real person. Too much edit removes the traits that make the text sound human.

- **Specific, unusual detail that is difficult to invent.** Examples are a real address, an unusual quote, or "the lawyer who used to work upstairs from my dentist." Models make specific details general. Human writers keep them.
- **Mixed feelings and tension with no solution.** "I think this is mostly good, but it bothers me and I can't fully explain why." Models usually give one clear opinion.
- **A writer who changes their opinion in the text.** The last third of the text knows something that the first third did not know.
- **References to a specific time.** Examples are slang, memes, or private jokes from a specific year and group. Models know these references a year or more after people use them.
- **Editorial decisions that the writer can explain.** If the writer can give the reason for a cut or a word, that is strong evidence of a human writer.
- **Real irregularity.** Examples are a long digression, a one-sentence paragraph, a comment in parentheses, or sections of very different lengths. A machine makes all parts the same.
- **Different sentence lengths.** Real writing changes sentence length irregularly. AI writing usually keeps a regular, medium sentence length.
- **Self-corrections and comments in parentheses.** "(I keep wanting to say 'almost' here, but it really was certain.)" Models do not frequently stop their own sentences.
- **All text from before November 30, 2022.** OpenAI released ChatGPT to the public on that date. Older text is almost never AI text.

## Important warning

Do not use the list above as a list of traits to add. Use it only to keep traits that are true.

These are examples of invented content: an admission that is not true, a hedge that is only decoration, and a number without a source. A one-sentence paragraph that you add for effect is also invented content. Invented content is a fabrication. It is also the next AI pattern that readers will learn to see. A reader quickly sees that added human traits are false. The writer must get these traits from real research and real thought. If the writer does not have them, do not add them.

## Check for too much edit

After a large edit, ask this question: does the result read like *this writer*, or like *an edited AI draft*? Edited AI drafts have their own style:

- All parts are equally clear and direct.
- All paragraphs have the same weight and structure.
- One short sentence and one long sentence occur in a regular sequence.

Readers can also see this style. If the edit made this style, put irregularity back into the text. Use the original lines of the writer to do this. Do not invent new irregularities.
